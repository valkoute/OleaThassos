Cookie Policy

Last updated: 15 August 2026

What are cookies?

Cookies are small files or similar technologies stored on a visitor's device. Under Greek rules implementing EU ePrivacy requirements, non-essential cookies generally require prior informed consent, while narrowly defined technically necessary cookies can be exempt.

Cookies used by this website

Necessary: The website may store a small preference indicating whether the visitor has made a cookie choice. This is used to remember the choice and is not used for advertising.

Optional analytics: None are enabled in the current version. If analytics such as Google Analytics, Matomo, Meta Pixel, or another service is added later, it must be added to the optional category and loaded only after the visitor gives the relevant consent.

Third-party content: External services such as maps, videos, social-media widgets, or booking platforms may set their own cookies when opened. Where possible, this site uses ordinary links rather than embedding those services.

Managing your choices

You can reopen the cookie settings at any time using “Cookie settings” in the footer. Optional cookies are off by default. Accepting optional cookies and rejecting them are presented as equally available choices.

Contact

For questions about cookies or privacy, contact the property owner using the details in the Privacy Policy.

The exact cookie list must be checked again after the site is deployed because the hosting provider, CDN, analytics tools, embeds, fonts, and other third-party services can change what is actually stored or transmitted.
